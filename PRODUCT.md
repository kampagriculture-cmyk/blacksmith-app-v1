# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Mixed context: operators log production at fixed shop devices (a tablet or PC per machine — SG-01, SG-02), used by whoever is running that shift. Supervisors and QC check status and approve from their own phone or desk. The job to be done is recording accurate start/checkout data for each production run — machine, knife, lot, quantities, defects, stone changes, tuning rounds — without slowing the shift down.

## Product Purpose

Replaces the shop's prior Google Sheet-based production tracking with a structured logging system. Enforces data integrity at the point of entry — a machine can't be double-booked with an open work order, defect counts can't exceed total output — where the spreadsheet allowed silent errors to slip through. Success is fewer bad or missing entries, and supervisors having real-time visibility into what's running without walking the floor.

## Positioning

The only system that stops bad data at entry — can't double-book a machine, can't log more defects than were produced — while giving supervisors live visibility into what's running, not just a digitized copy of the old sheet.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, trustworthy. It should never leave an operator doubting whether an entry saved, and errors should explain what to fix rather than just reject the input.

## Anti-references

Should not read as a digitized paper form or a spreadsheet with a UI bolted on. It needs to feel like real, considered software — clear hierarchy, confident interaction — not a Google Forms clone.

## Design Principles

Floor-entry speed and supervisor visibility carry equal weight; neither is sacrificed for the other. Bad data is prevented before submission, not discovered after — client-side checks mirror the backend's own integrity rules so mistakes are caught in the moment. An operator is never left wondering whether an action succeeded; every save gets clear, immediate confirmation. Errors explain what to fix, not just that something failed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific WCAG level requested. Standard readability bar: sufficient contrast and legible type sizes for a bright shop environment, comfortable for imprecise or gloved tapping on shared devices.
